7. NOTES
   - Heat-wave labels are runs of days, so the label of D+1 also depends on D+2 (Stage 3).
   - Positive shares grow from train to test (hotter recent summers); compare models on the same split.
   - A Random Forest cannot predict values above those seen in training (leaves store averages).
   - Correlated features (e.g. feels_like_mean / feels_like_max / lags) share their importance.
   - The test set is small (2 summers), so the test numbers are noisy.
